# Chat Library: find the work you left unfinished

## What this is

You have had many chats with Claude. Some ended with a job still open, such as a question Claude asked you or a step you meant to do later.

Chat Library finds those chats for you. It sorts every chat by topic and hides the automatic robot runs that clutter your history.

It runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Open it

1. Click your **Chat Library** bookmark. The address is `http://127.0.0.1:8010/library.html`.
2. If the page does not load, go to **If something is wrong** at the bottom.

## Find what is still to do

1. Click the **Tasks left** tab. It is open when you arrive.
2. Look at the coloured label on each card. It tells you why the chat is on the list.

| Label | Colour | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Unfinished plan** | Red | Claude made a to-do list and some items were never ticked off. |
| **Waiting on you** | Orange | Claude's last message asked you a question or needed your decision. |
| **Open items noted** | Blue | Claude's last message mentions something still to do. |
| **Follow-up proposed** | Purple | Claude suggested a follow-up chat that you never started. |

3. Read the bullet points on the card. They are Claude's own closing words from that chat.
4. Click **Open chat** to pick the work back up.
5. When the job is finished, click **Mark done**. If it was never a real job, click **Not a task**.

The list is a good guess, not a promise. A later chat may already have finished the job. Check before you mark it done.

## Narrow the list

- **Topic:** click a coloured topic on the left, such as Identity & Access or Network & Connectivity.
- **Type:** tick one or more types, such as Fix / Troubleshoot or Plan / Strategy.
- **Last active:** choose how far back to look. The default is the last 60 days.
- **Search:** type in the search box, or press the `/` key.
- **Star:** click the star on a card to pin it to the top.

The four coloured boxes at the top are shortcuts. Click **Waiting on you** to see only the chats where Claude is waiting for an answer from you.

## Share your list with someone

1. Click **Copy checklist**.
2. Paste it into an email, a Teams message or a ticket.

This is the only safe way to share. Never send the chat files themselves.

## Look at everything

- **All chats** shows every conversation, grouped by topic. Repeated chats are folded into one card.
- **Automated runs** shows the robot tasks that run on a schedule. You can ignore it unless you are curious.
- **Personal chats** are hidden. Tick **Personal chats** on the left to show them.

## Keeping it up to date

The page refreshes every Monday at 7:00. A Mac that was asleep catches up when it wakes. IT sets this up once.

## Privacy: please read

- The page keeps a private copy of your chats on your computer.
- That copy contains everything you typed, including anything you pasted, such as a password.
- Do not email, upload or share the folder. Use **Copy checklist** instead.
- **Mark done** is remembered only in your browser on this computer.

## If something is wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page will not open | Ask IT to check that Chat Library is running. |
| A new chat is missing | It appears after the next Monday refresh. Ask IT to refresh it now. |
| A chat is under the wrong topic | Tell IT. They can fix it in one line. |
| **Tasks left** looks wrong | Click **Not a task**. The flags are a best guess. |
